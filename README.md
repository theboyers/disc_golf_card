# Disc Golf Card

> **Transparency Note:** This is an entirely AI-generated application. I am not hiding that fact, and I intend to keep the app up to date over time.

Disc Golf Card is a simple, focused app built to do one thing well: **score your disc golf rounds and view or share your past rounds**—without clutter, distractions, or unnecessary features.

Requires Android 8.0 or newer.

---

## What It Does

- **Score Your Round:** Quickly and easily keep track of your scores hole by hole while you play.
- **View Past Rounds:** Browse your round history and view any past round in a clean, easy-to-read layout.
- **Share Easily:** Share your round results in a clear, readable format.
- **Easy on Your Battery:** Designed to use as little battery as possible so your phone lasts through your round.
- **Take Your Data With You:**
  - **Export Anywhere:** Export your round history in a format you can open and use in other tools.
  - **Seamless Device Switching:** Moving to a new phone? Transfer your full round history effortlessly.
  - **Future-Proof Imports:** Exports from any older version of the app can always be imported into any newer version.

## Using Disc Golf Card

**Start a round.** Enter the course and optional layout, choose 9, 18, 27, or a custom number of holes, add up to eight players, and optionally set an overall par or a starting hole. If you start on hole 14 of 18, the app follows 14 through 18 and then wraps around to 1 through 13.

**Score each hole.** Tap − or + next to each player. Running totals update as you go, and Previous / Next move through the round in course order. Finish on the last hole.

**See the result.** The summary ranks the card, shows every player's total, and—if you set an overall par—shows how each player finished relative to it.

**Review past rounds.** History lists your saved rounds; tap one to see the full scorecard. Use the share button to send the results, or delete a round you no longer need.

**Resume an unfinished round.** If you leave the scorecard mid-round, your scores are kept. Open the app again to resume at the last hole you played or discard the unfinished round.

## Your Data

- Rounds are stored on your phone. There is no account and no cloud requirement.
- **Export** your history from History → ⋮ → **Export to Drive**. Choose Google Drive or any folder in the system file picker.
- **Import** that file on the same or a new phone. Rounds already on the phone are skipped, so importing twice will not create duplicates.
- If Android backup is enabled and you sign in with the same account, the app's data can transfer automatically when you set up a new device.
- Deleting a round from History removes it permanently.

## Privacy

No accounts, ads, trackers, analytics, or location access. The app does not request internet access.

## What It Doesn't Do

Disc Golf Card intentionally avoids extra features—no social feeds, no ads, no GPS tracking, and no bloat. Everything in the app exists solely to help you score rounds, view and share your history, and keep your data portable.
